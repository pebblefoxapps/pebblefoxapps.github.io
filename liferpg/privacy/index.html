LifeRPG Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

LifeRPG is made by Pebblefox ("we", "us"). This policy explains what LifeRPG collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email pebblefoxapps@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Your account. Your email address and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never readable by us), or your Google account's email and name if you sign in with Google. Your display name, your daily goal and your time zone. We use these to run your account and show your days correctly.

Your focus activity. Your sessions (when they started and ended, how long they ran, the activity and its category), your routines (names, lengths and reminder times), your streak and rest days. We use these to save your progress and show your rhythm.

Squads, if you join one. Your display name, your focus time this week and whether you're focusing right now are shown to the members of that squad. The squad's name and invite code are shared with whoever you give them to.

What stays on your phone

  • Session protection. If you turn it on, LifeRPG uses Android's Usage Access to see which app is open, and "Display over other apps" to cover the apps you chose to block. The list of apps on your phone, your choices and what you open are used only on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Reminders, the widget and the countdown notification are created on your phone.
  • Offline sessions are saved on your phone until they can sync to your account.
  • A session log of timer events (no names, emails or notes) stays on your phone. It is only sent if you choose to share it from Settings.

Ads

LifeRPG shows one small banner ad after a finished session, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device details and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first using Google's consent message. If you don't consent, LifeRPG shows no ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Who helps us run LifeRPG

  • Supabase stores your account and progress, and handles sign-in.
  • Google provides Google sign-in (if you use it) and AdMob ads.
  • Expo delivers app updates; your phone contacts their servers to check for updates.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. We don't sell your personal data, and LifeRPG has no third-party analytics or tracking beyond the ads described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Delete account, which removes your account and its data from our service. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it.

Children

LifeRPG is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and each account can only read its own data (and what its squads share). No method is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Important changes will be mentioned in the app.
